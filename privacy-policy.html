1. Summary

Arrow Escape is a puzzle game. Your game progress (levels, stars, coins, boosters and settings) is stored only on your device. We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number or precise location.

2. Information collected by third-party services

The app uses the following third-party services, which may collect data under their own policies:

  • Google AdMob (ads): may collect the advertising ID, IP address, device information and ad interaction data to show and measure ads, and to personalise ads where permitted. See Google's advertising policy.
  • Google Play Billing (in-app purchases): purchases are processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment card details. We only receive confirmation that a purchase was completed.
  • Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics: collect app usage events, crash logs, device model, OS version, app version and an anonymous app instance ID, to improve stability and gameplay. See Firebase privacy information.

3. How we use information

  • To run and improve the game and fix crashes.
  • To show ads that support the free game.
  • To process and restore purchases.

4. Your choices

  • You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in Android Settings > Google > Ads.
  • You can delete all local game data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.
  • Where required by law (for example in the EEA, UK or Switzerland), the app asks for your consent before showing personalised ads.

5. Children

The app is not directed to children under 05 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will help remove it.

6. Data sharing and retention

We do not sell personal information. Data is shared only with the service providers above. Local game data stays on your device until you delete it. Analytics and ad data are retained by those providers according to their policies.

7. Security

We rely on Android's app sandbox and the security of the providers named above. No method of electronic storage is completely secure.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version. Continued use of the app means you accept the updated policy.
